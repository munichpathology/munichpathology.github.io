Advancing pathology with interpretable, clinically reliable AI.

The Munich Center for Computational Pathology unites pathologists, AI scientists, and disease experts from TUM, LMU, and Helmholtz Munich to develop trustworthy AI for medical imaging, diagnostics, and translational research.

Collaborative network LMU, TUM, Helmholtz Munich, and regional clinical partners share expertise, knowledge, and data. This ensures high quality research and cutting-edge science.
Research focus Pathology foundation models, interpretable AI, and real-world clinical application are only three of our focuses. Our research interests covers the full spectrum of medical and diagnostic AI.
Goal Deliver AI tools that support diagnosis, therapy selection, and patient care. Discover new, actionable insights for improved treatments strategies through AI-driven research.
